The writing is the interface

No card, no border, no toolbar. Newsreader at 19 px on 33 px leading, one measure, sitting directly on paper. The prompt is Instrument Serif italic and drops to 42% opacity the moment you type — it opened the door, it doesn't need to stand in it.

The mood input is the mark

Not a slider, not five faces. The same bloom the timeline will draw, growing under your thumb. There are only 25 states, so all 25 geometries are built once at mount and indexed during the drag — no path maths on the gesture path.

What the drag feels like

The lattice fades in only while you're dragging, so the pad is quiet at rest. Each step crossing fires a spring on the bloom and a selection haptic — you can set your mood without looking at the label.

It gets out of the way

With the keyboard up the picker collapses to a single line: the bloom at 30 px and its two words. Tap it to bring the pad back. The composer never scrolls the writing off screen to make room for a control.

Saving is not a button

Autosave debounced at 800 ms, then a whisper in the corner: 48 words · Saved on this device. That line is doing double duty — it is also the privacy promise, repeated quietly every day.
