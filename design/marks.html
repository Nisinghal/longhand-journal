Mood system · draft 02

Five ways to draw a day

The colour field encoded the data and none of the feeling. A flat fill is a dashboard cell; this app is about handwriting and attention, so the mark should look made rather than computed.

Colour still does the same work — valence moves the hue along the dawn arc from the last draft. What changes is that energy is now carried by drawn form: how open the flower is, how tall the stem, how dense the scrawl. Two channels instead of one, and the second one is handmade.

Every mark below is generated from the same (valence, energy, seed) your entries already produce, so the month is deterministic — the same day always draws the same way — but no two days are identical. All five run on the same data. Pick one and I will build the composer's mood picker as that mark, live under your thumb.

Where I'd go

Bloom if you want the app to be loved and screenshotted — it is the most distinctive thing on this page and it is your instinct, drawn out. Scrawl if you want it to be respected: it is the only one where a month genuinely looks like a page you wrote, and it never risks reading as decoration. Stitch is the sleeper — it is the only mark that makes the week continuous rather than 30 separate objects, which is closer to how a week actually feels.

What I'd avoid

Mixing two. The mark is the app's signature; two systems reads as indecision, and the mood picker can only teach one language.

The picker follows the mark

Whichever wins, the composer's mood input becomes that mark growing under your thumb — drag up for energy, across for valence, and the flower opens or the stem climbs as you go.

Still true from draft 01

Hue arc unchanged, chroma still capped, no red. Skipped days stay hollow. Colour never carries meaning alone — every mark keeps its text label.

Cost

All five are react-native-svg paths generated on the fly — no image assets, no shaders. A month is 30–35 small paths, which renders fine. Scrawl is the heaviest at roughly 8 paths a day.
